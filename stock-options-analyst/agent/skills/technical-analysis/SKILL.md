# Technical analysis checklist

For each shortlisted ticker, classify: **strong bullish / moderately bullish / neutral-consolidating / moderately bearish / strong bearish**. Explain price structure — do not rely on a single indicator.

## Trend
20 / 50 / 100 / 200-day moving averages and their relationships.

## Momentum
RSI(14), MACD, relative strength vs S&P 500, volume confirmation, price momentum.

## Levels
Support, resistance, prior highs/lows, breakout/breakdown levels with volume confirmation.

## Volatility
Historical vol, ATR, recent expansion vs contraction — feed this into IV regime judgment.

Technicals confirm or deny timing; they do not replace cycle + fundamental + options valuation pillars.
