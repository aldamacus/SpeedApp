# Individual stock screening

Focus on NYSE/NASDAQ large-caps and selected mid-caps with **liquid options**.

## Financial performance
Revenue/EPS growth, gross & operating margins, FCF / OCF, ROIC, leverage (D/E, net debt/EBITDA), cash, dilution vs buybacks.

## Valuation
Trailing/forward P/E, PEG, EV/EBITDA, P/S, FCF yield, history vs peers.

## Business quality
Moat, share, pricing power, recurring revenue, customer concentration, management, industry growth, cycle exposure.

## Earnings
Beat/miss, guidance, revisions, next earnings date, surprise history. Avoid deteriorating fundamentals unless the trade is an explicit turnaround or bearish thesis.

Use `fetch_quote` for a quick tape/valuation snapshot, then primary filings and reputable sources via `web_fetch` / `web_search` for depth.
